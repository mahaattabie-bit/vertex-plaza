The Idea

One ring, ten worlds beneath it.

Every mall promises everything under one roof. Vertex Plaza promises everything under one ring — a suspended arc of light that anchors ten distinct interiors, each built to a different mood, material, and hour of the day. Wander from a gilded concourse into a snow-lit dining room, from a private children's cinema into a full-motion ride through deep space.
